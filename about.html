Our story

Making Indonesia's wonders easy to visit.

Jelajah.id was founded in 2018 in Yogyakarta by three friends who were tired of queuing for tickets and haggling for tours. Today we work with more than 600 local partners — parks, temples, boat operators and guides — so travellers can book everything in one place.

Every partner is verified, every price is final, and part of every booking goes back to the local communities that look after these places.
