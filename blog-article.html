Arrive early, dress warm and pick the right viewpoint — here is how to get the most out of Bromo's famous sunrise.

Start at 3 AM

Jeeps leave Cemoro Lawang between 3 and 3.30 AM. Book a day tour that includes pick-up so you don't have to negotiate in the dark.

Choose your viewpoint

Penanjakan 1 is the most famous but also the busiest. King Kong Hill and Bukit Cinta offer almost the same view with fewer people.

Pack layers

Temperatures can drop to 5°C before dawn. A jacket, gloves and a buff make the wait far more enjoyable.

Stay for the crater

After sunrise, ride across the sea of sand and climb the 250 steps to the crater rim before the tour buses arrive.
