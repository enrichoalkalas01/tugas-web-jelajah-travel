This Privacy Policy explains how PT Jelajah.id Wisata Indonesia ("Jelajah.id", "we") collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use our website and services.

1. Data we collect

Name, email, phone number, nationality and payment details you enter when booking, plus technical data such as device type, browser and pages visited.

2. How we use your data

To process bookings, send e-tickets and reminders, provide customer support, prevent fraud and — only with your consent — send promotions.

3. Sharing with partners

We share only the data needed to fulfil your booking (such as name and number of guests) with the destination or tour operator. We never sell your data.

4. Payment security

Payments are processed by licensed payment gateways. Card numbers are encrypted and never stored on our servers.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and measure how the site is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@jelajah.id.
