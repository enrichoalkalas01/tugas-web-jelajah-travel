We want you to book with confidence. This policy explains when you can cancel and how refunds are paid.

1. Free cancellation

Cancel up to 24 hours before your visit date for a 100% refund on entrance tickets and day tours.

2. Holiday packages

Packages can be cancelled free up to 7 days before departure. Between 7 and 3 days a 50% refund applies; within 3 days no refund is given.

3. Cancellations by the partner

If a destination closes or a tour is cancelled because of weather or safety, you receive a full refund or a free reschedule.

4. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3–7 working days. E-wallet refunds usually arrive within 24 hours.

5. Rescheduling

You can change your visit date once for free, up to 24 hours before the original date, subject to availability.
